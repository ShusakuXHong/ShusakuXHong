About the hotel

Practical comfort, Chom Chao style.

Tucked into Sangkat Chom Chao 2 on the western edge of Phnom Penh, the hotel serves travelers passing through Dangkao district — airport arrivals, road trips out to the provinces, and guests who'd rather skip the noise of the riverside for something calmer.

Every one of the 50 rooms is air-conditioned with a flat-screen TV, private bathroom, and free in-room Wi-Fi. Ask at the desk for airport transfer or a rental car, and the laundry service will have your clothes turned around before you check out.
